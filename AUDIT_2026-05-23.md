## 0. Executive verdict

**We are saturated on the current paradigm.** The R-042 LB 0.3866 (R-034
PAIR + rule_override post-process) is, within ±0.001, the realistic LB
ceiling we can reach by re-arranging the components we currently own.
After R-027 PAIR (2026-05-13), 7 LB-tested experiments produced 2 wins
and 5 losses. The 5 losses are not random noise — they map cleanly onto
3 toxic classes (B-impure, B-meta-by-association, B-player-style) that
the data structurally rejects. Every "small OOF lift, big LB hope"
candidate built since R-042 (R-058 v15feat_c_oldtest_avg3, R-059
v15feat_a_oldtest_avg3, R-060r recvprofile, R-061r recvhand, R-064
spin) is either already disconfirmed by class or has marginal OOF and
high transfer risk.

**The honest gap to close**: top-10 is 0.4445+, top-3 is 0.49+, our
R-042 is 0.3866. The 0.06–0.10 gap is structural, not parameter-search
shape. Teammate's "pure-legal ceiling" estimate after stripping their
SGP-leak and pseudo-target tricks is ~0.4149 — still +0.028 above us
and the realistic upper bound for any clean approach. Our remaining
+0.028 of headroom requires NEW model classes, not more swaps.

**The 3 highest-EV legal preflights to open** (all UNVERIFIED on our
pipeline; treat upper-bound numbers as teammate attributions, not
point predictions):
1. **R-066 AutoGluon clean component** — never built; redirected R-031
   budget into SoftF1 (toxic carrier) instead. Single highest-EV legal
   preflight on the board. Teammate attributes +0.01 LB to their
   `best_quality` preset, but on our pipeline the first LB datapoint
   determines actual transfer. Strict legal-data contract required (no
   `train_pseudo.csv`, no SGP overwrite, match-grouped CV, OOF + test
   probability arrays).
2. **R-069 Path B causal LM smoke** (1h GPU) — only untested
   structural axis in the zoo. R-013 smoke 2026-05-10 already showed
   correlation < 0.85 vs all zoo components. Either it adds a
   genuinely decorrelated leg (potentially +0.005–0.015 LB via blend
   ADD), or it confirms saturation. Either way the answer is
   informative. No full 30h run until smoke artifacts are reviewed.
3. **R-068 action-point combo features (isolated ablation)** — 2
   cheap categorical features on top of v15feat_a, B-feature class,
   ISOLATED only (no bundling with score-pressure or oldtest or
   seed-avg). Closest thing to the R-034 winning recipe that's not
   already explored.

(R-067 score-pressure features are conditional on a feature-overlap
audit vs v15feat_c, since v15feat_c was R-058 closed-negative and may
already represent score-pressure. R-070 soft-F1 on v15feat_a action
head, R-071 5-seed averaging, R-072 player-profile re-test are
deferred — see §7 priority matrix.)

**The things we should stop immediately:**
1. More B-feature swap experiments around `v14_seed2_v15feat_*`. Every
   nearby variant (R-058, R-059, R-064) has now produced marginal OOF
   or LB-failed. The v15feat_a swap was a specific local maximum, not
   a recipe.
2. Higher-order (9c/10c) blend search until the pool is purged of
   v11_mulminet variants. `higher_order_blend_search.py` keeps finding
   v11_mulminet at top — that's the LB toxicity, not a feature.
3. Bayes/COBYLA weight refinement on any blend with LB-untested
   components. R-055 showed it converts small risks into −0.0141
   cliffs.
4. R-064 5-fold launch on spin features. Smoke OV −0.0001 base /
   −0.0045 opt / AUC −0.0063. Predicted lift is below noise; LB
   ratio risk is high; compute would be better spent elsewhere.
5. Consensus pseudo-label V2c reopens. Codex BLOCK / ABANDON is
   correct — pool size, monoculture, and trainer plumbing are all
   broken simultaneously.

We have ~2 LB slots per day to spend (3 limit minus the unused
teammate slot). That is ~40–60 slots before the competition ends.
At our current spend efficiency (2 wins / 5 losses since R-027), most
slots are being spent disconfirming bad ideas. The next 7 days
matter more than the 13 after.

## 5. Real bottlenecks (root-cause analysis)

### 5.1 actionId rare-class F1

The dominant Loop class (cls 1) is at F1 ~0.45–0.55. Rare classes
(Arch cls 8, Smash cls 3, Lob cls 14, Knuckle cls 9) are at F1
0.08–0.26. Macro-F1 weighting means the bottom 5 classes dragging by
~0.30 each cost ~0.10 macro-F1, or ~0.04 OV at competition weight 0.4.

**Mechanism**: cross-entropy loss biases predictions toward common classes.
LightGBM and transformers share this bias. SoftF1 loss is the textbook
fix; R-031 attempts this on v11_mulminet_aug_oldtest (banned family).
Even if soft-F1 lifts the standalone metric, the carrier component is
LB-toxic.

**Better attack**: apply soft-F1 to v14_seed2_v15feat_a action head, not
